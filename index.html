NetworkPolicy Intelligence System (NPI)

Jan – May 2026
End-of-studies engineering project · NextStep IT × TEK-UP

A six-layer security observability platform for OpenShift/Kubernetes. It consumes cluster-native signals — OVN ACL drop logs, live NetworkPolicy objects, time-series flow statistics — and converts them into structured findings, ranked anomalies and plain-English explanations.

  • Detection — three tiers: Z-score spikes over Welford baselines, deterministic rules, and Isolation Forest for multivariate outliers.
  • Policy audit — live NetworkPolicy objects checked against an 8-rule catalogue, scored per namespace and graded A–F.
  • On-cluster LLM — llama3.2:3b on a dedicated tainted node, CPU-only Q4 inference, 3.8 s time-to-first-token, zero data egress.
